# Contributing to FootBrake

Thanks for helping! Issues and pull requests are welcome on GitHub.

## Ethos

FootBrake is free software in the spirit of [HandBrake](https://handbrake.fr): no ads, no
monetisation, no telemetry. FootBrake will always respect its users, and contributions that
change this won't be accepted.

## Licensing of contributions

FootBrake's code is licensed under the GNU General Public License v3 (see `LICENSE`).

**By submitting a contribution (a pull request, patch or similar), you agree that:**

1. your contribution is licensed under the GNU General Public License, version 3; and
2. you additionally permit the FootBrake maintainer to distribute your contribution, as part
   of FootBrake, through app stores (including Apple's App Store and TestFlight) under those
   stores' terms of service, even where those terms are incompatible with the GPL; and
3. you have the right to make this contribution (it is your own work, or you have permission
   to submit it under these terms).

Point 2 exists because Apple's App Store terms are widely considered incompatible with the
GPL. It lets the official FootBrake be published there with your contribution included, while
everyone else's rights under the GPL stay exactly the same.

The pull request template asks you to confirm this with a checkbox.
