# Gauss privacy policy

_Last updated: 15 November 2026_

Gauss does not collect, store or share any personal data.

## What Gauss does with your videos

Gauss works on your iPhone. When you trim, compress or convert a video, the processing
happens on the device. Gauss does not upload your videos or any other information, and
it has no accounts, analytics, advertising or tracking.

## Permissions

- **Choosing a video:** Gauss uses the system Photos picker and Files browser, so it only
  sees the video you pick.
- **Saving to Photos:** Gauss asks for permission to add videos to your library. It can
  add videos but cannot read your library.

## Sharing

Exported videos are only shared when you choose to, using the standard iOS share sheet. What
happens next is up to the app or service you share with.

## Changes

Any changes to this policy will be published in this file, in Gauss's public source
repository at https://github.com/nnathan/Gauss.

## Contact

Questions can be raised as an issue at https://github.com/nnathan/Gauss/issues.
